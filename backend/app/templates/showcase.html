Active 5 s cadence telemetry.

2. STALE

Last reading retained and flagged stale.

3. OFFLINE
Age ≥ 90.0 s

Node disconnected; isolation active.

4. INVALID
Echo Out-of-Range

Emits null; zero fabrication blocked.
