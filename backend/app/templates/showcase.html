Missing-Data Reconstruction Laboratory Benchmark

Strict separation of Setting A (Online Causal Streaming: Zero Future Leakage) vs. Setting B (Offline Retrospective Gap Reconstruction).

Setting A: Online Causal Streaming

Operates in real-time when packets drop. Evaluates strictly on historical observations prior to the gap (x_{k-1}). The future boundary point x_{k+m} is completely unknown.

- LOCF: Flat persistence baseline (MAE=0.110cm at 15s)
- Causal Trend: Velocity + exponential decay (MAE=0.064cm at 15s)
- Physics Twin: Forward RK4 integration from last known state (MAE=0.023cm, 79% improvement)

Setting B: Offline Retrospective Gap Bridging

Operates after communication is restored. Has access to both the past observation x_{k-1} and future observation x_{k+m}.

- Linear Interpolation: Two-sided boundary bridging (MAE=0.042cm at 15s)
- At long gaps (>60s), Linear Interpolation excels because future point pins the end.
