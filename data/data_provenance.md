# CivicTwin-R — Data Provenance & Environmental Observation Registry

Geographical Scope: **Chennai Metropolitan Basin, Tamil Nadu, India**  
Basin Geographic Bounds: Latitude 12°40'N to 13°40'N, Longitude 79°10'E to 80°25'E; Total Area: 4,974 km²  
Sub-Basins represented in twin model: **Adyar**, **Cooum**, **Kosasthalaiyar**

---

## 1. Provenance Labeling Standard

Every data point traversing CivicTwin-R carries an explicit provenance tag to eliminate confusion between physical measurements, simulations, and model projections:

| Canonical Tag | Definition | Usage in CivicTwin-R |
|---|---|---|
| `WOKWI_SIMULATED` | Readings generated by the Wokwi virtual ESP32 and simulated HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor. | Telemetry emitted from Wokwi browser simulation. |
| `PYTHON_SIMULATED` | Readings generated by the headless Python multi-zone sensor simulator. | Headless synthetic sensor pipeline for automated tests and CI. |
| `OBSERVED` | Real measurements whose source, instrument, and observation time are verified. | Official CFM-DSS ARG rain gauge records and reservoir daily storage metrics. |
| `REPLAYED_HISTORICAL` | Previously recorded environmental observations streamed at a controlled rate for testing. | Cyclone Michaung Dec 2023 hourly storm series replayed through the pipeline. |
| `DIGITAL_TWIN` | Values calculated, simulated, or predicted by the digital twin physics and forecasting engine. | Discrete-time mass-balance zone levels, scenario outputs, and rate-of-rise forecasts. |
| `IMPUTED` | Missing readings reconstructed by an imputation method (e.g., LOCF, linear interpolation). | Held-out recovery benchmark evaluations during sensor dropouts. |

> **Strict Rule:** Simulated, predicted, replayed, or imputed values are **never** labeled as live observations.

## 2. Dataset 1: Automatic Rain Gauge (ARG) Records

- **Original Source & Dataset Title**: Chennai Flood Monitoring and Spatial Decision Support System (CFM-DSS) — Real-Time Data Acquisition System (RTDAS) ARG Network.
- **Official URL**: [https://www.chennaifloodmonitor.tn.gov.in/](https://www.chennaifloodmonitor.tn.gov.in/)
- **Supervising Agencies**: Government of Tamil Nadu, Commissionerate of Revenue Administration (CRA), Greater Chennai Corporation (GCC), Water Resources Department (WRD), SECON-JBA JV, IIT Madras.
- **Retrieval Date**: October 23, 2026.
- **Station Count vs. Record Count**:
  - **Monitoring Stations**: 117 distinct automated rain gauge stations across Chennai sub-basins (Adyar, Cooum, Kosasthalaiyar, Kovalam, Vegavati, Kiliyar, Araniyar).
  - **Observation Records**: 328 historical snapshot records captured in the raw portal dashboard payload (`data/raw/cfm_dashboard_raw.json`).
- **Measurements & Units**: Rainfall (`rainfall_mm`) and prior-period rainfall (`previous_rainfall_mm`) in millimetres (mm).
- **Temporal Metadata**: ISO-8601 timestamps in Indian Standard Time (IST, UTC+05:30).
- **Physical Sensor Note**: These stations use tipping-bucket or optical rain gauges. **They record rainfall precipitation, NOT river water level or river stage.** Rainfall informs the catchment inflow in the digital twin, but is not treated as a direct water depth reading.
- **Data Classification**: `OBSERVED` (historical snapshot from official network).
- **Storage Location**:
  - Raw: `data/raw/cfm_dashboard_raw.json`
  - Cleaned: `data/processed/chennai_arg_rainfall.csv`

## 3. Dataset 2: Chennai Major Reservoirs & Lakes Storage Capacity

- **Original Source & Dataset Title**: CFM-DSS Lake Operation Guidance System (LOGS) & WRD Hydrological Reports.
- **Official URL**: [https://www.chennaifloodmonitor.tn.gov.in/](https://www.chennaifloodmonitor.tn.gov.in/)
- **Supervising Agencies**: Water Resources Department (WRD), Government of Tamil Nadu.
- **Retrieval Date**: October 23, 2026.
- **Monitored Reservoirs**:
  1. `TNCH-07-T0726` — **Chembarambakkam Reservoir** (Adyar Basin upstream regulator; Gross: 103.22 MCM, Live: 42.45 MCM, 41.12% fill)
  2. `Po001` — **Poondi Reservoir** (Kosasthalaiyar Basin upstream regulator; Gross: 91.49 MCM, Live: 13.82 MCM, 15.10% fill)
  3. `RD001` — **RedHills / Puzhal Lake** (North-Central Chennai drinking water supply; Gross: 93.45 MCM, Live: 68.33 MCM, 73.12% fill)
  4. `Cho001` — **Cholavaram Lake** (Kosasthalaiyar tributary; Gross: 30.61 MCM, Live: 4.08 MCM, 13.32% fill)
  5. `TK-001` — **Thervoy Kandigai Reservoir** (Tiruvallur storage; Gross: 14.16 MCM, Live: 6.91 MCM, 48.80% fill)
- **Measurements & Units**: Gross storage capacity (`MCM`), Live storage capacity (`MCM`), Dead storage (`MCM`), Storage fill percentage (`%`). 1 MCM = $10^6 \text{ m}^3$.
- **Sensor Clarification**: Storage volumes are derived by WRD hydrological staff from reservoir staff gauges and stage-storage (elevation-capacity) rating curves. They should **not** be assumed to use automated capacitive or hydrostatic pressure transducers unless specifically verified.
- **Data Classification**: `OBSERVED` (official daily hydrological telemetry).
- **Storage Location**:
  - Raw: `data/raw/cfm_dashboard_raw.json`
  - Cleaned: `data/processed/chennai_reservoirs.csv`

## 4. Dataset 3: Cyclone Michaung Extreme Rainstorm Hourly Series

- **Original Source & Dataset Title**: ECMWF ERA5 Reanalysis calibrated against IMD ground precipitation records for Cyclone Michaung.
- **Official URL**: Open-Meteo Open Climate Historical Archive (`https://archive-api.open-meteo.com/v1/archive`)
- **Geographic Coordinates**: Latitude 13.0827°N, Longitude 80.2707°E (Chennai City Centre / Nungambakkam-Saidapet centroid).
- **Event Period**: 2023-12-01T00:00 to 2023-12-06T23:00 (144 continuous hourly observations).
- **Measurements & Units**: Hourly precipitation (`mm/hr`) and Cumulative rainfall (`mm`). Peak hourly rate: 26.1 mm/hr; total storm rainfall: 369.8 mm.
- **Pipeline Replay Role**: When replayed through `scripts/replay_historical.py`, these historical rainfall observations are used to drive a scaled runoff proxy curve to test the IoT ingestion pipeline under extreme surge conditions.
- **Important Distinction**: The proxy water level generated during replay is a calibrated simulation curve for pipeline testing, **not** an in-situ river stage gauge measurement.
- **Data Classification**: `REPLAYED_HISTORICAL`.
- **Storage Location**:
  - Raw: `data/raw/chennai_michaung_hourly_raw.json`
  - Cleaned: `data/processed/chennai_michaung_hourly.csv`

## 5. Unverified Data Sources & Technical Limitations

| Claim / Source | Status | Limitation & Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| In-situ real-time river stage ultrasonic sensors in Adyar/Cooum rivers | `UNVERIFIED` | No public real-time continuous river depth API is provided by GCC/WRD. Real-time river depth is simulated by the digital twin based on rainfall runoff and lumped mass-balance. |
| Direct physical ESP32 deployment in Chennai waterways | `PROTOTYPE ONLY` | ESP32 devices and HC-SR04 sensors are demonstrated in laboratory / Wokwi virtual emulation. They are not physically installed in Chennai rivers. |
| Certified flood warning thresholds for Chennai | `PROTOTYPE ONLY` | The 70% (warning) and 85% (critical) thresholds are software test parameters and are not official government flood alert lines. |
| Live 2D finite-element hydrodynamic simulation | `EXCLUDED` | CivicTwin-R explicitly uses a lumped discrete-time mass-balance model ($V_{t+1} = V_t + \Delta t(Q_{\text{in}} - Q_{\text{out}})$), not an uncalibrated 2D hydrodynamic hydraulic finite-element solver. |
