# Scientific Limitations & Operational Scope

**CivicTwin-R is an academic cyber-physical and hydrological research platform. It must not be used for certified emergency forecasting, flood evacuation directives, or municipal warning operations.**

1. **Digital Surface Model (DSM) vs. Bare-Earth DTM:** The terrain grid derives from Copernicus GLO-30 (`30 m` native DSM resampled to `150 m` in `EPSG:32644`). Unlike surveyed bare-earth LiDAR DTM, raw DSM pixels include rooftop and tree canopy elevation bias in dense neighborhoods.
2. **Coarse Grid & Lumped Subsurface Drainage:** The `150 m` overland grid and spatially lumped nominal drainage sink (`mm/hr`) abstract away sub-grid street curbs, storm drain pipe diameters, pump station schedules, and culvert capacities (no SWMM subsurface hydraulics).
3. **Lack of Surveyed Flood-Depth Calibration Rasters:** No open post-event flood depth rasters or physical river-stage gauge time-series exist in the integrated Chennai datasets (`chennai_arg_rainfall.csv` measures atmospheric rainfall in `mm`, not channel water level). Reported modeled flood depths and inundation polygons are scenario screening estimates, not field-validated ground truth.
4. **Non-Spatial vs. Spatial Rainfall Corpora:** The preserved `chennai_arg_rainfall.csv` (`328` rows, `13` subbasins) contains no geographic coordinates and is confined to non-spatial analytics. Spatial map markers use exclusively the `56` coordinate-bearing CFM-DSS WFS stations.
5. **Simulated Bench IoT Nodes:** Dual-node MQTT telemetry (`node_01`, `node_02`) originates from the software-simulated ESP32/JSN-SR04T tank simulator (`scripts/mqtt_node_simulator.py`) and remains strictly isolated from Chennai urban hydrological routing. Physical ESP32/Wokwi firmware is provided under `firmware/` for hardware-in-the-loop deployment but was not physically wired in hardware tests.
6. **Offline Static Assets & OSM Coverage:** Building geometries in `osm_buildings_south.geojson` are capped (`18,198` named, multi-storey, civic, and core hotspot footprints) for smooth offline browser rendering; missing OSM height attributes use explicitly tagged typology fallbacks (`height_source = "type_fallback_estimate"`).
7. **Historical Secret Hygiene Notice:** Prior Git commits before `b0fb8de` included default development InfluxDB credential strings (`civictwin-dev-token` / `civictwin_local`) in environment/docker templates; current templates require user-generated `.env` credentials (`setup.sh`). Any local tokens reused outside loopback should be rotated.
